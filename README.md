## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on the end-to-end analysis of **31,000+ retail transactions** from the Vrindavan Fashion Store. As a Math and Stats graduate, my goal was to demonstrate how raw, inconsistent data can be transformed into high-level business intelligence through rigorous cleaning and statistical summarization.

## 🏗 Data Engineering & Cleaning (The Process)
Before any analysis could begin, I performed extensive "Data Wrangling" to ensure mathematical accuracy:

*   **Categorical Normalization:** Used logical functions to standardize Gender (mapping 'M/W' to 'Men/Women') and Order Status labels.
*   **Type Casting:** Resolved data integrity issues where numerical quantities were entered as strings (e.g., "One" converted to 1) using `SWITCH` and `VALUE` functions.
*   **Temporal Logic:** Engineered a custom sorting index to force a chronological January-to-December flow, preventing the default alphabetical sorting in Pivot Tables.
*   **Scale Normalization:** Applied a **1,000,000 scale factor** to revenue axes to improve dashboard readability and data-to-ink ratio.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights
The final dashboard provides four critical layers of insight:

1.  **Sales vs. Volume Trend:** Identified a significant peak in **March**, followed by a stable revenue plateau throughout Q2 and Q3.
2.  **Customer Demographics:** Analysis revealed that **Women drive 64.2% of total revenue**, identifying a clear target market for inventory planning.
3.  **Fulfillment Health:** Calculated a **92.2% delivery success rate**, with "Cancelled" and "Returned" orders identified as key areas for operational cost reduction.
4.  **AOV (Average Order Value):** Established a benchmark AOV of **$689.29**, a vital metric for tracking customer spending growth over time.
